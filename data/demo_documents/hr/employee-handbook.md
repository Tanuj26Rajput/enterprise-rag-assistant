# Helix Labs Employee Handbook

Owner: People Operations, Helix Labs
Edition: 2026.2
Applies to: all employees of Helix Labs GmbH

## Welcome

This handbook describes how Helix Labs works day to day: the hours we keep,
the benefits you have, and what we expect of each other. It is not an
employment contract. Where this handbook and your contract disagree, your
contract governs.

## Working hours and flexibility

The standard working week is 38 hours. Core hours, when you are expected to
be reachable, are 10:00 to 15:00 in your registered working time zone.
Outside core hours you arrange your day as suits you and your team.

Time zones are a first-class constraint here. If your team spans more than
four hours of time difference, the team agrees a written collaboration
window and records it in the team charter; nobody should be taking meetings
at 22:00 as a matter of routine.

## Pay and review

Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month, or the preceding working day
when the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday. Payslips are available
in the people system from the 23rd.

Compensation is reviewed once a year, in March, for everyone. Promotions are
decided separately and can happen at any point in the year; a promotion
carries a salary change effective from the first of the following month.

## Benefits

- **Pension**: Helix Labs contributes 5 percent of gross salary, and matches
  employee contributions up to a further 3 percent.
- **Health**: private health insurance for you, with the option to add
  dependants at the company rate.
- **Learning budget**: 1,500 EUR per calendar year for courses, books,
  conferences and certifications. It does not carry over.
- **Home office allowance**: 750 EUR on joining, and 400 EUR every two years
  thereafter, for desk, chair, monitor and peripherals.
- **Equipment**: a laptop of your choosing from the standard catalogue,
  refreshed every three years or sooner if it fails.

## Leave

Annual leave, sick leave, parental leave and unpaid leave are described in
the separate Leave Policy, which is the authoritative document. The summary
here is for orientation only: full-time employees receive 28 days of annual
leave per year plus public holidays.

## Probation

New employees have a six-month probation period. During probation the notice
period is two weeks on either side. There is a structured check-in at one
month, three months and five months; none of these should contain a surprise,
because feedback is given as it arises rather than saved up.

## Expenses

Work expenses are reimbursed with the monthly payroll when submitted by the
15th. Anything submitted later is reimbursed the following month. Expenses
over 250 EUR need manager approval before the spend, not after.

Travel is booked through the company travel system. Economy class for
flights under six hours; premium economy above that. You keep any loyalty
points you earn.

## Conduct and grievances

The Code of Conduct sets out the behaviour expected of everyone here and the
process for raising a concern. If you have a problem with a colleague, with
your manager, or with the company, you can raise it with your manager, with
any member of People Operations, or anonymously through the reporting form.
Every report is acknowledged within two working days.

## Leaving

The notice period after probation is one month for individual contributors
and three months for managers and above, on either side, unless your
contract states otherwise.

On your last day, return company equipment and hand over your work. Access
to systems is withdrawn at the end of your final working day. People
Operations will arrange a departure conversation; it is optional, and what
you say in it is not shared with your manager without your agreement.

## Changes to this handbook

People Operations maintains this handbook. Material changes are announced to
all employees at least 30 days before they take effect, except where a legal
change requires a shorter timeline.
