# Remote Work Policy

Owner: People Operations, Helix Labs
Effective: 1 March 2026

## 1. How we work

Helix Labs is remote-first. There is no expectation that anyone works from
an office, and no advantage, in pay or in promotion, to doing so. Teams are
distributed by default and the company's processes are written for that.

Remote-first has a specific meaning here: if a decision is made in a room,
it is not a decision until it is written down where everyone can read it. A
meeting that produces no written outcome has produced nothing.

## 2. Where you may work

You may work from anywhere in the country of your employment contract
without notifying anyone.

Working from another country requires approval, because it creates tax and
employment obligations for the company. Approval is routine for short
periods and is handled by People Operations.

- Up to 30 days per calendar year in an EU or EEA country: notify People
  Operations before you travel; approval is automatic.
- 31 to 90 days, or any period outside the EU or EEA: request approval at
  least six weeks in advance.
- More than 90 days: this is a relocation, not remote work. See section 6.

Do not work from a country under sanctions, or from one where the company
has no legal ability to employ you. People Operations maintains the current
list.

## 3. Office space

Helix Labs maintains offices in Berlin and Lisbon. Both are open to any
employee, with desks bookable through the office system. There is no
requirement to use them.

If you prefer to work from a co-working space, the company covers up to
250 EUR per month against a receipt. This is in place of, not in addition
to, a desk in a company office.

## 4. Equipment and environment

The home office allowance in the employee handbook covers desk, chair,
monitor and peripherals. Equipment bought with the allowance belongs to you.

You are responsible for a working internet connection suitable for video
calls. The company contributes 40 EUR per month towards connectivity costs,
paid with salary and requiring no receipt.

Your working environment must allow you to work confidentially. Do not take
calls about customer data in a shared space where you can be overheard, and
use a privacy screen when working from a public place.

## 5. Meetings and availability

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your registered working time zone, as set
out in the employee handbook.

Recurring meetings are scheduled inside the overlap window agreed by the
team. A meeting outside someone's core hours needs their agreement each
time it happens, not once when it was first scheduled.

Cameras are optional. Nobody is required to explain why their camera is off.

## 6. Relocation

Moving to a different country permanently requires a new employment
arrangement and is only possible where Helix Labs has an entity or an
employer of record. Start the conversation with People Operations at least
three months before you intend to move.

Salary is set by role and level, with an adjustment band by country. A
relocation may therefore change your salary in either direction; People
Operations will tell you the new figure before you commit to anything.

Moving within the same country does not change your salary and needs only
an address update.

## 7. Security while remote

The information security policy applies in full wherever you work. In
particular: full-disk encryption on, screen lock at five minutes, no
customer data on personal devices, and no production access over a network
you do not control without the company VPN.

## 8. Visitors to the office

Guests are welcome in either office and must be signed in at reception.
Guests are not left unattended in areas with screens showing customer data.
