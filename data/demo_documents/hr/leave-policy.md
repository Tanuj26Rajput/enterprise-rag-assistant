# Leave Policy

Owner: People Operations, Helix Labs
Effective: 1 January 2026
Supersedes: Leave Policy 2024.1

## 1. Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
in addition to the public holidays of their registered work location. Part-time
employees receive a pro-rated entitlement, rounded up to the nearest half day.

Leave accrues monthly at one twelfth of the annual entitlement. An employee
joining mid-year receives the entitlement for the remaining whole months of
that year.

### Carry-over

Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried into the following
calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March and cannot be carried
further or paid out. Anything above 5 days is forfeited at the end of the
calendar year.

The limit is deliberate. Leave exists to be taken, and a large accrued
balance is usually a sign that someone has not been able to take time off,
which is a problem to solve rather than a balance to bank.

### Requesting leave

Requests go through the people system and are approved by your direct
manager. Give as much notice as you reasonably can; as a guide, two weeks
for one to four days, and six weeks for anything longer.

A manager may decline a request only for a stated operational reason, and
must offer an alternative period. A declined request is escalated to People
Operations if no workable alternative can be found.

### Leave on departure

Untaken accrued leave is paid out on departure at the normal daily rate.
Leave taken in excess of what has accrued is deducted from the final salary.

## 2. Public holidays

Public holidays follow the employee's registered work location. An employee
required to work on a public holiday receives a day in lieu, to be taken
within three months.

## 3. Sick leave

Report sickness to your manager as early as practical on the first day of
absence. There is no fixed limit on paid sick leave for ordinary illness.

A medical certificate is required from the fourth consecutive working day of
absence, and must be sent to People Operations rather than to your manager.

Sickness during annual leave converts to sick leave on production of a
medical certificate, and the affected annual leave days are returned to your
balance.

## 4. Parental leave

Birth and adoptive parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave,
regardless of gender, to be taken within 12 months of the birth or
placement. This is in addition to any statutory entitlement in the
employee's work location; where the statutory entitlement is greater, the
statutory entitlement applies.

Parental leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks, each of at least
two weeks. Annual leave continues to accrue throughout.

A returning parent may request a reduced schedule for up to six months at
the corresponding pro-rated salary, and return to their previous schedule
afterwards without reapplying.

## 5. Compassionate leave

Up to 10 days of paid leave following the death of a close family member, and
up to 5 days for a serious illness in the immediate family requiring the
employee's presence. This leave is granted by People Operations and does not
require a manager's approval.

No employee should be negotiating with a manager about bereavement. Tell
your manager you are taking compassionate leave and inform People Operations
when you are able.

## 6. Unpaid leave and sabbatical

Unpaid leave of up to three months may be requested after two years of
service, subject to business approval. Benefits continue; pension
contributions pause for the duration.

After four years of continuous service, employees may request a sabbatical
of up to eight weeks at half pay. One sabbatical may be taken every four
years.

## 7. Study and volunteering

Two days per calendar year of paid study leave for examinations related to
your role, and two days per calendar year of paid volunteering leave for a
registered charity of your choice.

## 8. Questions

People Operations owns this policy. Where this policy and local employment
law conflict, local law applies and People Operations will confirm the
treatment in writing.
